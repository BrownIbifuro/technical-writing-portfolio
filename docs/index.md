## My Approach to Technical Writing

I believe good technical documentation should do more than describe how something works. It should help its intended audience understand, apply, and troubleshoot the information presented.

My approach prioritises:

- Accuracy and technical clarity.
- Logical information architecture.
- Consistent terminology and formatting.
- Practical examples and reproducible instructions.
- Audience-focused communication.
